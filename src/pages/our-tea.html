Why CTC

Consistency, strength and colour in the cup.

CTC — crush, tear, curl — produces a granular leaf style that infuses quickly and delivers a consistent, full-bodied cup.

That consistency is valuable in blended and packaged products, where a dependable base grade matters as much as the origin story behind it. It is why CTC grades are widely used by tea packers, blenders and private-label brands.

Because the tea is processed and graded at origin, questions about a grade can be answered by the team that produced it.
