Nyamasheke, Rwanda

A single-origin tea house in the highlands of Rwanda.

Cyato Tea Plantation & Factory Ltd cultivates, processes and manufactures tea in Nyamasheke District, in the western highlands of Rwanda — a landscape of rolling green hills, volcanic soils and cool mountain air.

We work from field to finished grade: fresh leaf is received at our factory, withered, processed using CTC technology, oxidised, dried and sorted into commercial grades. Our teas are supplied to importers, packers, blenders, distributors and private-label brands worldwide.

From field to factory

A controlled journey from fresh leaf to finished grade.

Every stage at our Nyamasheke facility has a purpose: protecting the leaf, developing its character and delivering consistency in the cup.

01

Fresh leaf reception

Leaf arrives from the plantation and is received with care to protect freshness.

02

Withering

Controlled air movement gently reduces moisture and prepares the leaf for processing.

03

CTC processing

Crush, tear and curl transforms the leaf into the granular style associated with CTC teas.

04

Oxidation

Carefully managed conditions develop colour, body and the character of the finished tea.

05

Drying

Heat is applied to stabilise the leaf and lock in the qualities developed upstream.

06

Sorting and grading

Finished tea is sorted into commercial grades to match different market requirements.
