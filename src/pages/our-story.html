Who we are

A tea business that begins with the land.

Cyato Tea Plantation & Factory Ltd cultivates, processes and manufactures tea in the highlands of Nyamasheke, in the west of Rwanda.

Our work spans the full journey of the leaf. Tea is grown in the plantation, delivered fresh to our factory, processed using CTC technology and sorted into commercial grades for buyers in different markets.

That continuity — from the field to the finished grade — is what allows us to speak precisely about our tea and to work closely with the trade customers who buy it.

The place

Nyamasheke: hills, mist and volcanic soil.

Nyamasheke sits in Rwanda's western highlands, a landscape of steep green hills, smallholder plots and tea gardens that disappear into low cloud in the early morning.

Altitude, cool air and volcanic soils create the conditions that give Rwandan tea its naturally bright colour and full body. The land here is not background to the tea — it is the reason for its character.

Working in this environment demands care. Cultivation practices are chosen to protect the soil, the water and the long-term productivity of the plantation.

The people

Knowledge carried by hand, harvest after harvest.

Tea is a crop that rewards attention. The selection of fresh shoots, the timing of the harvest and the way leaf is handled before it reaches the factory all shape the tea that follows.

Our people bring that knowledge to every stage — from the plantation to the processing floor. Their experience is the bridge between the growing environment and the consistency our trade customers expect.

Processing at origin means the same team that grows the leaf also sees it through manufacture and grading. Responsibility for quality is not handed over halfway.
